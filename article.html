From Axim to the World: Theophilus Annan's Journey in Building TheoCodesx Studio

By TheoCodesx Newsroom | Axim, Ghana

THEOCODESX STUDIO

In an era where digital media is transforming how people communicate, tell stories and promote businesses, a young Ghanaian entrepreneur is working to create opportunities for local voices through creative media.

Theophilus Annan is the founder of TheoCodesx Studio, a creative media and digital content production studio based in Axim, Western Region, Ghana.

Building a Creative Media Platform

TheoCodesx Studio focuses on photography, videography, podcast production, live streaming, graphic design, video editing, event coverage and digital content creation.

Giving Local Voices a Platform

The studio seeks to provide opportunities for young people, emerging talents, businesses and community organisations to share their stories and showcase their abilities.

"The Voice of the Streets, The Power of the Mic."

A Vision Beyond Axim

The long-term vision is to establish TheoCodesx Studio as a recognised creative media brand, support local talent and contribute to the growth of the creative industry.
